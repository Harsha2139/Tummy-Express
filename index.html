One kitchen. Many hungry customers.

A restaurant has several riders waiting at different places and several orders to send out. Every wrong choice costs kilometres, minutes and money.

The problem

Match riders to orders, find the cheapest dish on the menu, and design the shortest drop route through every customer.

Why it matters

Delivery apps make millions of these decisions daily. A smarter algorithm saves fuel, time and customer patience.
